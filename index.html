How it works

1. You confirm you own the domain  →  2. Interstice scans  →  3. You get a clean HTML report to send to your client / team.

pip install requests

python scanner.py --domain example.com --confirm-ownership

⚠️ Only scan assets you own or have written permission to test.

Why Interstice?

Most scanners are scary, expensive, or made for hackers. Interstice is made for website owners — simple, defensive, and honest. No jargon. Just: what's missing, how bad is it, and how to fix it.
